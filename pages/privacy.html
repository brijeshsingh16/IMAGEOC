Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

IMAGEOC is built with privacy at its core. Every image and PDF is processed directly inside your browser without uploading your files to any server.

Privacy First

Local Processing

IMAGEOC processes every image and PDF entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device, giving you complete control and privacy.

No File Uploads

We never upload, copy or store your files on any server. Everything remains on your own device throughout the editing process.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

IMAGEOC does not require registration or user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or personal documents.

Uploaded Files

Uploaded images and PDFs stay only in your browser's temporary memory and disappear automatically when you refresh or close the website.

Cookies & Preferences

Theme Preference

We may save your Light or Dark theme preference locally in your browser so your preferred appearance is restored on future visits.

No Tracking Cookies

IMAGEOC does not use tracking cookies to monitor your activity, analyze your files or collect personal information.

Security

Browser-Based Security

Because every tool works locally in your browser, your files remain under your control from upload to download.

No Cloud Storage

IMAGEOC does not operate cloud storage or maintain databases containing your uploaded files.

Third-Party Resources

Open Source Libraries

IMAGEOC uses trusted browser libraries such as Cropper.js, PDF-Lib, jsPDF, Google Fonts and Font Awesome to provide its features.

No Data Sharing

These libraries run completely inside your browser and never receive or transmit your uploaded files.

Policy Updates

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect new features, improvements or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about privacy or data security, please visit our Contact page. We're always happy to help.
