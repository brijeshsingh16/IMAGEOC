Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Find answers to the most common questions about IMAGEOC, image editing, PDF tools, privacy, browser support and more.

Privacy & Security

Is my data uploaded?

No. IMAGEOC never uploads your files. Every image and PDF is processed directly in your browser, ensuring complete privacy.

Is everything processed locally?

Yes. All editing, conversion and compression happens directly on your device without sending data to any server.

Image Tools

Which image formats are supported?

IMAGEOC supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP and other popular image formats depending on your browser.

Can I compress PNG images?

Yes. PNG, JPG and WEBP images can be compressed while maintaining excellent visual quality.

PDF Tools

Can I merge PDF files?

Yes. Multiple PDF documents can be combined into a single PDF directly inside your browser.

Can I rearrange PDF pages?

Absolutely. Drag and reorder pages before downloading your updated PDF.

General

Is IMAGEOC free?

Yes. IMAGEOC is completely free to use with no account, subscription or installation required.

Does IMAGEOC work offline?

Once the website has loaded, most tools continue working without an internet connection because all processing happens in your browser.

Browser Support

Which browsers are supported?

IMAGEOC works on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Opera and most modern Chromium-based browsers.

Can I use IMAGEOC on mobile?

Yes. IMAGEOC is fully responsive and works smoothly on phones, tablets and desktop devices.

Downloads

Where are my processed files saved?

Files are downloaded directly to your device using your browser's default download location.

Can I edit a downloaded file again?

Yes. Simply upload the file again and continue editing using any supported IMAGEOC tool.
